# Open questions

| OQ-01 | 2026-10-21 | `identity.api_client_secrets` (data model §3) has no `tenant_id` column, unlike every other client-owned table in the schema — it's reached only through `api_client_id` → `identity.api_clients.tenant_id`, which does have RLS. Is the missing `tenant_id` intentional (join-scoped access is enough, since nothing should ever query `api_client_secrets` directly without going through its parent), or a gap that should get its own `tenant_id` + RLS policy? | Task 1.7 (API clients: create, rotate, revoke) | Intentional — no new column. Every `app/apiclients.go` query against `api_client_secrets` joins through `api_client_id` to `api_clients` (and runs inside `db.Pool.WithTenantTx`), so `api_clients`' own RLS already scopes which secrets are reachable; nothing in this codebase queries `api_client_secrets` standalone by its own `id`. (1.7, 2026-10-23) |

What should `tools/migrate-v3` actually read? | Task 1.13 (v3 importer: users and profiles) | Two CSV files, `users.csv` (`id,email_address,full_names,password`) and `profiles.csv` (`user_id,msisdn,network`; `network` read and discarded), column names copied verbatim from data model §3. A placeholder, not a confirmed export format — revisit the moment real v3 export access exists; the parsing is isolated to `tools/migrate-v3/csv.go` so a different real format is a small, contained change. (1.13, 2026-10-23) |
| OQ-03 | 2026-10-23 | OIDC single sign-on (task 1.15, for v3 clients with `clients.allow_okta_login`): data model §3 gives `identity.sso_connections` (`protocol`, globally unique `domain`, `config` jsonb "metadata URL, client ID; secrets in KMS") but nothing says (1) how a tenant proves it owns a `domain` before "users at this domain sign in by SSO" applies — without verification (e.g. a DNS TXT record), whoever registers a domain first, including a public one like `gmail.com`, takes over sign-in for everyone at it; and who may create a connection (tenant admin or staff); (2) which tenant a connection binds to, when real multi-user tenants don't exist until task 2.2 (1.7's "creating user's ID" placeholder doesn't fit a connection shared by many users); (3) whether a first SSO sign-in creates a user (JIT), links to an existing password/passkey user with the same email, or is refused; whether password/passkey sign-in stays allowed for an SSO domain; and whether the IdP's MFA satisfies step-up (1.6); (4) how the IdP client secret is stored — `platform/crypto.Envelope` like TOTP secrets, inside `config` or a separate column. | OQ-04 | 2026-11-02 | Reseller and sub-account tenants (task 2.2b): the design gives the schema (`tenants.type`, `parent_tenant_id`) and the requirements say the Financial tier unlocks "reseller accounts", but nothing says (1) how an account becomes a reseller; (2) who can get into a sub-account; (3) what tier a sub-account starts on; (4) which country a sub-account can be in. | Task 2.2b | Answered by the user (2026-11-02): (1) a staff action, only for an active direct account on the Financial tier (an app use case in 2.2b; the staff route comes with 2.9); (2) the reseller's active members reach its sub-accounts through the parent link, with no copied memberships; the client's own users join through 2.3's invitations; (3) sandbox, then its own KYC; (4) the reseller's country, currency, time zone and region. Following from these: only two levels (a sub-account never becomes a reseller), and a reseller that drops below Financial or is suspended or archived keeps its sub-accounts but can't add new ones. |
| OQ-05 | 2026-11-02 | Memberships, roles and invitations (task 2.3): the data model gives `roles` (built-in when `tenant_id` is NULL; "Admin, Developer, Finance, Viewer ...", permissions like `{sms:send, payouts:approve}`) and `memberships.status` (invited/active/removed, `user_id NOT NULL`), but nothing lists the permissions or the roles' contents, says whether tenants get custom roles, or says how someone is invited by email when accounts doesn't know emails and `memberships` needs a user. | Task 2.3 | Answered by the user (2026-11-02): four built-in roles over a small catalogue (`account:read/manage`, `members:read/manage`, `credentials:read/manage`, `billing:read/manage` reserved); Admin `*`, Developer credentials + read, Finance billing + read, Viewer read-only. No custom roles yet. Invitations: accounts asks identity over gRPC to find or create the user by email (identity creates an unverified user as sign-up does and sends the invite), the same answer whether or not the email existed; the invitee accepts after signing in. Split into 2.3a (roles, permissions, member management) and 2.3b (invitations). |
| OQ-06 | 2026-11-02 | Service catalogue (task 2.4): the data model gives `services`, `service_country_rules` ("country pack overrides") and `tenant_services` (selected/enabled/suspended); system design §2 says each service declares a minimum tier per country and a locked service shows the tier it needs. Nothing lists the services, says whether the pack or the table holds the country rules, what a demotion does to an enabled service, or whether a service can be removed. | Task 2.4 | Answered by the user (2026-11-02): the requirements' tier table (sms, surveys, ussd at starter; whatsapp, rewards at business; payouts, collections at financial; sender IDs, shortcodes and dedicated USSD codes are Phase 12 applications); the pack holds each country's rules and accounts syncs them into `service_country_rules` at startup; a demotion returns a service to selected (locked, `service.locked`) and the tier regained re-enables it; an account may remove a selected service (`service.deselected`). |
| OQ-07 | 2026-11-02 | KYC (task 2.6): system design §2 fixes one provider interface and OD-03 defaults to a fake adapter, but nothing says who starts a check and whose identity it verifies, what each result does automatically, how a vendor's webhook gets past the gateway's session check, or how the fake vendor behaves. | Task 2.6 | Answered by the user (2026-11-02): an Admin (account:manage) starts an individual check verifying themselves; a passed individual check promotes sandbox → starter, nothing auto-demotes, and a decided check is final except review → decision; the webhook goes through the gateway on a route the spec declares public (security: []), verified by the adapter's signature check; the fake vendor is a real HTTP server whose decisions a test drives. Recorded in ADR 0008. |
| OQ-08 | 2026-11-03 | KYB (task 2.7): the requirements ask for a registry lookup (Kenya BRS or the local equivalent), tax PIN, directors and proof of address, but there's no registry access in this environment, and nothing says how a business check is decided, when an account may submit, or whether the profile can change after verification. | Task 2.7 (registry lookup adapter) | Answered by the user (2026-11-03): start with manual entry verified by staff, and integrate a registry in future; every KYB submission goes to the staff review queue (2.9 decides and promotes); submission needs the starter tier; the profile and owners lock while in review and after a pass, until re-verification exists. **Still open:** which registry integration (BRS, BRELA, URSB or an aggregator) and when; ROADMAP 2.7's "registry lookup adapter" waits for that access. |
| OQ-09 | 2026-11-03 | Documents (task 2.8): the design says "encrypted S3-compatible object storage" and documents are reused across applications, but not how files are encrypted, uploaded (the gateway caps bodies at 1 MB), which types and sizes are allowed, what scans them, or whether KYB needs them. | Task 2.8 | Answered by the user (2026-11-03): envelope encryption in the app (`platform/crypto.Envelope`, a documents KMS key), so the bucket only holds ciphertext; multipart upload through the API, up to 10 MB, PDF/JPEG/PNG judged from the bytes; ClamAV now (clamd's INSTREAM; a fake speaking the same protocol in tests); KYB needs the pack's required documents (KE: certificate of incorporation, tax certificate, proof of address). |
| OQ-10 | 2026-11-03 | Staff review (task 2.9): decision Q4 asks for "a minimal staff role check" with no console, but nothing says how a staff member is identified, which staff actions exist before the console (Phase 10), or what safeguards staff decisions need. | Task 2.9 | Answered by the user (2026-11-03): staff are users listed in a new `identity.staff_members` table, granted and revoked only by an ops CLI (`vaspro identity grant-staff` / `revoke-staff`), and checked over gRPC on every staff call so a revocation is immediate; one flat staff role until the console. Staff actions in 2.9: the review queue and decisions, unlocking a locked-out user, tier changes, making a reseller, and downloading an account's documents. No step-up and no self-review rule for now (revisit with the console). |
| OQ-11 | 2026-11-04 | Data residency (task 2.10): data model §4 gives only `tenants.region` ("data residency tag"); hosting is OD-02 (Phase 8/17) and per-tenant residency is task 21.4.
